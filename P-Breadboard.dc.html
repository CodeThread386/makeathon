The SELECT Makeathon is a flagship annual innovation program: a 36-hour challenge where students, designers and technology enthusiasts build real-world prototypes for problem statements from industry partners, across hardware and software.

19 to 20 January 2027 at Anna Auditorium, VIT Vellore, Tamil Nadu 632014.

Teams of 3 to 5 members: undergraduate students, research scholars and working professionals.

Free for VIT students. External participants pay ₹500 per person.

FaultWatch, Vitalis, Cosmolith, Live Wire, CogniForge and Open Innovation. Problem statements from industry partners will be released before the event.

A total prize pool of ₹1,00,000, awarded to teams. Every participating team receives digital certificates, and teams keep full ownership of their projects.

Write to enquiry@makeathon.in. We will share the partnership deck and the ways to get involved, from contributing problem statements to judging.
